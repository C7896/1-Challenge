Privacy Policy

1% Challenge · Effective August 22, 2026

1% Challenge gives you one small self-improvement challenge each day and lets you keep a private journal about it. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, where it goes, and how to delete it. It is short because the app collects very little.

Who we are

This app is published by Rustin Katsura. For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, contact rustinkatsura@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your name. The name you enter when you sign up. It is used to greet you inside the app and is shown only to you.
  • Your email address. Used only to create your account and sign you in.
  • A username. Free text you choose when you sign up. It is not verified and is not shown to anyone else.
  • Your journal entries. Your answers to "What did you do?" and "How did it make you feel?", along with that day's challenge text and a timestamp.
  • Your progress. Your current streak, longest streak, and total number of completed challenges.

That is the complete list. The app asks for nothing else.

Where it is stored

Your account and entries are stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC, which acts as our data processor. You can read Google's privacy terms at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Who can see your entries

Only you. Your journal entries are private to your account. Our database security rules prevent any other user from reading them. Entries are never made public, never shown to other users, never sold or shared, and never used to train any model.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking tools of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No artificial intelligence or machine learning applied to your entries.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a single daily reminder at 9:00 AM. This is a local notification created on your own device. No push token, device identifier, or any other information is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn reminders off at any time in your device Settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and entries until you delete them. You can delete everything yourself, at any time, from inside the app: open the home screen and tap Delete account. You will be asked for your password to confirm. This permanently removes your login and every journal entry you have written, and it cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app for any reason, email rustinkatsura@gmail.com and we will delete your data on request.

Children

1% Challenge is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR in the European Economic Area and the UK, and under the CCPA in California, you have the right to access your data, correct it, and have it deleted. The in-app Delete account control satisfies the right to deletion immediately and in full. For access or correction requests, email us at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date at the top of the page will be updated. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
